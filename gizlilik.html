Privacy Policy — Chess 2: King's Defense

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Chess 2: King's Defense is an offline-playable tactical chess defense game. No account registration is required and no personal data is collected on developer servers.

1. Local Data Storage

Your campaign progress, talent tree, Soul Gems, achievements, language, and audio/haptics preferences are stored locally on your device only.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version may display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads via Google AdMob. Users in the EEA/UK can manage consent anytime via Ad Privacy Options on the main menu.

3. In-App Purchases

Remove Ads and Soul Gem packs are processed securely by Google Play Billing or Apple App Store (StoreKit). We never access or store payment details.
